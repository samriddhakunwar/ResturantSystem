# PawPeek

Coloured cat paws (calico, ginger, black) reaching in from the right edge of the screen or a panel, as in the kit.

- Use them only on landing, empty-state and booking-confirmation screens. Don't put them on the menu, the cart or any staff screen.
- Place them with `position:absolute; right:0` inside a `position:relative` container. Use one to three of them, stacked with space between.
- They are pure decoration: `alt=""` and `pointer-events:none`, and they must never cover a button or text.
- The kit's doodles (paw trail, cup, yarn ball, toy mouse, hearts, olive paw) are in the **Doodles** and **Paws** asset groups. Pair them with one script word.
